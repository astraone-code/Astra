Important: This does not mean you are immediately accepted. (Going through these steps does not mean guaranteed acceptance)

Requirements
  1. Active on Discord. (Announcements, war commands, boss timers, voice chat, hangout).
  2. Willing to join events: Boss Hunts, Alliance Wars, World Dungeons, PvP Events.
  3. Respectful, positive attitude. No toxicity or aggressive behavior.
  4. Fill out data sheets (item tracking and progression logs).
  5. Notify leaders in advance if you cannot attend or will be away.
  1. Combat Power: ASTRA 125k / ASTRO 100k (Subject to change)
Important Notes
  1. Joining Discord does not mean acceptance. Leaders review applications and notify approved players.
  2. After joining, leaders observe how you play with the group before deciding on reward rank eligibility.
