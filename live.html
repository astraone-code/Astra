What this cannot do

read first
⚠
One direction only. Edits made in the roster app never reach the Sheet. The Sheet is the source; this page is the reader.
⚠
Google caches published links. A change in the Sheet can take minutes to appear. Fetch again if a row looks stale.
⛔
Public means public. Anyone with the link can read every cell. Never put officer notes or private details in a synced Sheet.
◈
CORS is Google's call. Browsers may block the fetch even when the Sheet is public. The log shows which endpoint answered; the CSV fallback always works.
◈
No true simultaneous editing. Several admins editing the same Sheet at once is merged by Google cell-by-cell, last write wins. That is a Sheets behaviour, not this page's.
